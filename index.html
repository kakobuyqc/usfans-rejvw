What is a
USFans spreadsheet?

In this guide, a USFans spreadsheet means a product-link shortlist organized by category, with notes for sizing, photos and costs. This independent page helps you browse CAFANS and plan that shortlist; it does not provide an official USFans spreadsheet or a downloadable product database.

02 / The shortlist method

Browse less.
Compare better.

A useful USFans finds spreadsheet should help you remember why an item made the list. Keep observations specific enough to compare later.

Find a starting point on CAFANS ↗

  1. Choose one category and a clear purpose.

    Start with the item you need: daily shoes, a lightweight jacket, a matching set or a jersey. Note fit preferences and your target budget before collecting links.

  2. Save the exact product and variant.

    Record the listing URL, seller, color, size and visible item price. Keep similar-looking products separate when their materials or measurements differ.

  3. Check evidence and missing information.

    Read the size chart and material description. Look for relevant detailed photos, and mark unclear information as unknown instead of filling it in from appearance.

  4. Compare the complete purchase.

    Review any applicable service, delivery and payment charges alongside the item price. Recheck the listing and purchase terms before placing an order.

03 / Product-link notes

What belongs in your sheet?

Use these fields for a USFans product links spreadsheet that remains readable as your shortlist grows.

Suggested fields for comparing fashion finds
Field What to record Why it helps
Category & purpose Shoes, layers, headwear or accessories; intended occasion. Keeps comparisons relevant to your actual needs.
Product & variant Listing URL, seller, color, selected size and model reference if supplied. Reduces confusion between similar listings.
Fit evidence Seller measurements, measurement units and your own reference garment. Gives more context than a letter size alone.
Photos & materials Detailed images, stated composition and any unanswered questions. Separates visible evidence from assumptions.
Cost breakdown Item price, currency and any applicable fees or delivery estimates. Supports a more complete budget comparison.
Status & date Shortlisted, needs information or passed; date last checked. Shows which details may need another look.

A listed price is a starting point. It may exclude service fees, domestic delivery, international shipping, payment charges and applicable import costs. Check the charges and terms for your specific order and destination.

04 / Direct answers

Before you
follow a find.

Short answers to practical USFans spreadsheet questions.

Where do the category links go?

The category links open the corresponding CAFANS category pages. General browsing buttons open the CAFANS homepage. These links do not open a USFans account or complete a purchase.

How should I compare spreadsheet finds?

Compare the exact product variant, seller size chart, material description, detailed photos, listed price and any additional purchase or delivery costs. Record missing information before deciding.

Are the brand images proof of stock or authenticity?

No. The Nike, Louis Vuitton, Gucci and Hermès images are visual category references. They do not verify CAFANS inventory, seller authorization, product authenticity or brand affiliation.

Can a listed item price tell me the final delivered cost?

No. A listed item price may exclude service fees, domestic delivery, international shipping, payment charges and applicable import costs. Check which charges apply to your specific order and destination.

Does this page provide live prices or verified quality-control photos?

No. This page provides category links and comparison guidance. Check the destination listing and request relevant photos or measurements before relying on any product information.
